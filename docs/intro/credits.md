# Credits

CodeIgniter was originally developed by <a href="https://ellislab.com/" target="_blank">EllisLab </a>. The framework was written for performance in the real world, with many of the original class libraries, helpers, and sub-systems borrowed from the code-base of <a href="https://expressionengine.com" target="_blank">ExpressionEngine</a>. It was, for years, developed and maintained by EllisLab, the ExpressionEngine Development Team and a group of community members called the Reactor Team.

In 2014, CodeIgniter was acquired by the <a href="https://www.bcit.ca/" target="_blank">British Columbia Institute of Technology</a> and was then officially announced as a community-maintained project.

In 2019, the CodeIgniter Foundation was formed to provide a perpetual managing group separate from any other entity to help ensure the framework's future.
